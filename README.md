# NICE Computer Education – School of Coding (Yavatmal) 🎓
### Student Review Assistant, Reputation Management System & Admin Portal

A custom review generation and reputation management software suite built for **NICE Computer Education – School of Coding**, located in Wadgaon, Yavatmal, Maharashtra.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Architecture

### 1. 🎓 Student Review Assistant (`index.html`)
* **Client Branding Standards:**
  * Strict requirement applied: Only the main brand name **NICE** is set in **Arial Bold** (`#064E3B`).
  * Secondary branding (`COMPUTER EDUCATION`), headers, and body typography use the executive modern typeface **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
  * Original high-resolution institutional logo with subtle rounded border styling.
* **Expanded Course Selection:**
  * **School of Coding:**
    * **C & C++ Programming:** `C Programming` *(Separate Chip)*, `C++ Programming` *(Separate Chip)*.
    * **Java Technologies:** `Core Java Programming (J2SE)` *(Separate Chip)*, `Advanced Java (J2EE)` *(Separate Chip)*.
    * **Python & AI:** `Python Programming`, `Python & AI Basics`.
    * **Web & PHP Development:** `Web Development`, `PHP Programming`, `ASP.NET & VB.NET`.
  * **Professional & Technical Skills:**
    * **Technical & Design:** `Computer Aided Design (CAD)`, `Computer Hardware Troubleshooting`, `Graphic Designing`, `Hardware & Networking`.
    * **Accounting & Tally:** `Tally Prime with GST`, `Tally ERP 9 with Invoicing`.
    * **Excel & Productivity:** `Advanced Excel & MIS Reports`, `Office Productivity & Data Management`.
    * **MS-CIT & Fundamentals:** `MS-CIT Certification`, `Computer Fundamentals & Typing`.
* **Calibrated Line Length AI Reviews:**
  * **Short (2–3 lines):** Punchy, authentic, practical feedback.
  * **Medium (4–5 lines):** Comprehensive, classroom & lab focused.
  * **Detailed (7–8 lines):** In-depth review with curriculum, faculty guidance, and career highlights.
* **Smart Review Routing (Reputation Shield):**
  * **4 & 5 Stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐:** Generates high-impact positive reviews, copies to clipboard in 1 click, and seamlessly directs the student to the official Google Review page (`https://g.page/r/CTKrEmKbJP-dEBM/review`).
  * **1 to 3 Stars (Grievance Interception):** Caught privately inside the system and sent to the Director/Admin dashboard so negative feedback never affects the public Google rating!
* **Multi-Language AI Generator:** Instant toggle between **English**, **मराठी (Marathi)**, and **हिंदी (Hindi)**.
* **📸 Lab & Certificate Photo Upload:** Students can take a photo of their certificate, classroom, or lab practice to attach directly on Google Reviews (boosting Google Maps ranking).
